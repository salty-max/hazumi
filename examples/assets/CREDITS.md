# Art credits

The gallery draws on commercial art packs. They are here because they were
bought for it, and they are listed here because most such licences ask for
attribution and because a public repository should say whose work it is
showing.

## Oryx Design Lab — 16-bit Fantasy

`oryx_16bit_fantasy_world_trans.png`, `oryx_16bit_fantasy_creatures_trans.png`

© 2013 Oryx Design Lab · <https://www.oryxdesignlab.com>

Twenty-four pixel cells on a twenty-four pixel margin. Used by `tile-field`,
`characters` and the floor under `blood-mage`.

## Minifantasy — Blood Mage

`blood-mage/`

Krishna Palacio · Minifantasy series.

Six 32x32 sheets, four facings each, with the pack's own `AnimationInfo.txt`
for the frame timings. Used by `blood-mage`.

## Everything else

`dungeon-tiles.png`, `dungeon-sprites.png`, `hero.png`, `tiles.png`,
`swatch.png` and the dungeon audio are placeholders and test patterns made for
the repository. `tiles.png` and `swatch.png` in particular are diagnostics —
flat colour fields for checking atlas slicing — and should not be used to
dress a scene.
